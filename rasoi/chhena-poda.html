Chhena Poda

A caramelised baked cheesecake of fresh chhena, sugar, and cardamom — its name literally means “burnt cheese”.

1. A Happy Accident

Local legend says a confectioner in Nayagarh in the 20th century left sweetened chhena overnight in a warm oven. The next morning he found a delicious caramelised cake.

2. Baking

Kneaded chhena is mixed with sugar, semolina, and cardamom, then baked slowly for hours, traditionally wrapped in sal leaves, until the outside caramelises.

3. A Sacred Sweet

Chhena poda is said to be a favourite of Lord Jagannath and is offered during the Rath Yatra festival.
