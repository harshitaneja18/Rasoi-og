Crispy Stuffed Punjabi Aloo Paratha

There is no breakfast in North India more universally loved than an Aloo Paratha straight from the cast-iron tawa. Flaky and golden on the outside, soft and spicy within, and crowned with a generous melting slab of fresh white butter (Safed Makkhan).

Ingredients & Measurements

For the Paratha Dough:

  • ✓ 2 cups whole wheat flour (Chakki Atta)
  • ✓ 1/2 tsp salt
  • ✓ 1 tbsp pure ghee or oil
  • ✓ 3/4 cup lukewarm water (adjust for soft pliable dough)

For the Spiced Potato Stuffing:

  • ✓ 4 medium starchy potatoes (boiled, peeled & cooled)
  • ✓ 2 green chillies (finely minced)
  • ✓ 1-inch piece of fresh ginger (grated fine)
  • ✓ 1/2 cup fresh cilantro / coriander leaves (chopped)
  • ✓ 1 tsp carom seeds (Ajwain, rubbed between palms)
  • ✓ 1 tsp roasted cumin powder (Bhuna Jeera)
  • ✓ 1 tsp dried mango powder (Amchur) or chaat masala
  • ✓ 1/2 tsp red chilli powder & 1 tsp salt
  • ✓ Desi Ghee for roasting

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Knead a Pliable Soft Dough

    In a large mixing bowl (Paraat), combine wheat flour and salt. Gradually add water and knead for 6–8 minutes until you achieve a soft, smooth, non-sticky dough. Coat with 1 tsp ghee, cover with a damp cloth, and rest for 20 minutes to relax the wheat gluten.

  2. Prepare the Non-Watery Stuffing

    Mash the completely cooled boiled potatoes thoroughly with a potato masher or grate them to ensure zero hard lumps (lumps will tear the dough during rolling). Add minced green chillies, ginger, cilantro, ajwain, roasted cumin powder, amchur, chilli powder, and salt. Mix gently into a cohesive, flavorful filling. Divide into 4 large balls.

  3. Stuff & Roll with a Light Hand

    Pinch a ball of dough slightly larger than a golf ball. Roll into a 4-inch circle. Place a potato filling ball in the center. Bring the edges together like a money-bag pouch, pinch tightly at the top to seal, and flatten gently. Dust with dry flour. Using a rolling pin, roll from the center outward with light, uniform pressure into a 7–8 inch round disc.

  4. Roast to Golden Perfection on Tawa

    Heat a heavy tawa over medium-high heat. Place the rolled paratha onto the hot tawa. Cook for 1 minute until small bubbles appear on top. Flip over and apply 1 tsp ghee evenly. Flip again and apply ghee to the other side. Press the edges gently with a flat spatula; watch the paratha puff up into an airy balloon with golden brown crisp blisters.

Chef’s Golden Rule: Cool Potatoes First!

Never, ever mash warm or freshly boiled potatoes for paratha stuffing. Warm potatoes release condensation when mixed with salt, turning the stuffing gummy and wet, which causes the dough to tear open during rolling. Boil your potatoes hours ahead and let them cool completely in the refrigerator.
