Hyderabadi Biryani

The famous kacchi biryani of the Nizams: raw marinated meat and partly cooked basmati layered and dum-cooked together into a fragrant, spiced masterpiece.

1. The Nizam’s Kitchen

Hyderabadi biryani developed in the royal kitchens of the Nizams, blending Mughlai and Telugu traditions. The city’s cooks perfected over many styles.

2. Kacchi vs. Pakki

In the kacchi method, raw marinated meat is placed at the bottom and topped with 70%-cooked rice. Both finish cooking together under dum, which requires great skill to time correctly.

3. Serving

Accompanied by mirchi ka salan (chilli-peanut curry) and dahi chutney.
