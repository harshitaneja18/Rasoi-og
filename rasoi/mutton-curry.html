Mutton Curry

Slow-cooked goat meat in a rich onion-tomato gravy with whole spices — a dish with countless regional versions and a place at every family celebration.

1. Regional Styles

Bengal has the potato-filled kosha mangsho, Rajasthan the fiery laal maas, Kerala a coconut-pepper curry, and Punjab a rich, onion-heavy bhuna gosht.

2. The Bhuna Step

The secret is bhunao: frying onions, spices, and meat patiently until the oil separates, which builds deep flavour before any water is added.

3. Serving

Served with rice, roti, or paratha.
