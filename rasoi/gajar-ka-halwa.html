Gajar Ka Halwa

Grated red winter carrots slow-cooked in milk, ghee, and sugar, finished with cardamom and nuts — North India’s favourite winter sweet.

1. A Winter Ritual

Gajar ka halwa is made when the juicy red Delhi carrots arrive in winter. It is a staple at weddings and festivals such as Diwali and Lohri.

2. Slow Cooking

Grated carrots are simmered in full-fat milk until it evaporates, then roasted in ghee with sugar and khoya until rich and glossy.

3. Serving

Served warm, often with a scoop of vanilla ice cream or a dollop of khoya.
