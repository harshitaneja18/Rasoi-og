Homestyle Palak Paneer

A quintessential North Indian farmhouse creation, Palak Paneer marries the earthy, mineral sweetness of garden-fresh spinach with the creamy softness of Indian cottage cheese (Paneer). Master the secret ice-bath blanching technique to keep your gravy a stunning brilliant emerald green.

Ingredients & Measurements

  • ✓ 500g fresh tender spinach leaves (Palak), washed & stemmed
  • ✓ 250g fresh Paneer (cut into 1-inch cubes)
  • ✓ 1 medium onion (finely chopped)
  • ✓ 2 medium ripe tomatoes (pureed)
  • ✓ 2 green chillies & 1-inch ginger piece
  • ✓ 6 cloves fresh garlic (finely minced)
  • ✓ 1 tsp cumin seeds (Jeera)
  • ✓ 1/2 tsp turmeric powder & 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ✓ 1/2 tsp Garam Masala & 1 tsp salt
  • ✓ 2 tbsp pure desi ghee or butter
  • ✓ 2 tbsp fresh heavy cream (for finishing)
  • ✓ 1/2 tsp kasoori methi (crushed)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

  1. The Ice-Bath Blanching Secret

    Bring 4 cups of water to a rolling boil with 1/2 tsp sugar (sugar protects the green chlorophyll). Plunge the washed spinach leaves into the boiling water for precisely 2 minutes. Immediately remove using tongs and plunge into a bowl of iced water. This thermal shock halts cooking instantly, sealing in the bright emerald hue. Puree the cold spinach with 1 green chilli and ginger into a smooth puree.

  2. Sauté the Masala Base

    Heat 2 tbsp ghee in a kadhai or skillet over medium heat. Add cumin seeds; let them splutter. Add minced garlic and sauté until fragrant and light golden (do not burn). Stir in finely chopped onions and cook for 5 minutes until soft and translucent. Add tomato puree, turmeric, coriander powder, and salt. Cook until ghee separates from the tomato masala.

  3. Simmer the Spinach Gravy

    Pour the emerald spinach puree into the cooked tomato-onion masala. Stir gently to incorporate. Simmer uncovered on low heat for 4 to 5 minutes. (Covering the pan with a lid traps volatile plant acids which turn spinach a dull olive green, so keep the lid off!).

  4. Fold in Paneer & Finish with Cream

    Gently fold in the fresh paneer cubes. Simmer for 2 minutes just to warm the paneer through (overcooking makes paneer rubbery). Sprinkle garam masala, crushed kasoori methi, and swirl in fresh heavy cream. Serve hot with warm rotis, parathas, or jeera rice.

Chef’s Secret for Tender Paneer

If using store-bought paneer, soak the cubed paneer in a bowl of warm, lightly salted water for 15 minutes before adding to the gravy. This rehydrates the dairy proteins and makes the paneer exceptionally soft, pillowy, and tender.
