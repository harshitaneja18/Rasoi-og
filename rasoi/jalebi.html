Jalebi

Crispy fermented batter spirals deep-fried and soaked in warm saffron-cardamom syrup — best eaten piping hot.

1. Persian Roots

Jalebi is related to the Persian and Arab zalabiya and has been made in India for at least five hundred years, appearing in medieval Jain and Sanskrit texts.

2. Making Jalebi

A lightly fermented batter is piped in spirals into hot ghee, fried until crisp, then dipped briefly in warm syrup so it stays crunchy outside and juicy inside.

3. Serving

Enjoyed with rabri or warm milk, or with poha for breakfast in Indore.
