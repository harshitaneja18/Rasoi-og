Punjabi Rajma Chawal

Across Punjab, Delhi, and Jammu, Sunday afternoon belongs to Rajma Chawal. This supreme comfort meal combines tender red kidney beans simmered in a deeply caramelized onion-tomato gravy with steaming, fluffy basmati rice, sliced onions, and a dollop of pure desi ghee.

Ingredients & Measurements

  • ✓ 1.5 cups (250g) Kashmiri or Chitra Rajma (soaked overnight for 8 hours)
  • ✓ 2 large red onions (finely minced)
  • ✓ 3 large ripe tomatoes (pureed)
  • ✓ 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ✓ 2 green chillies (slit lengthwise)
  • ✓ 1 black cardamom, 2 green cardamoms, 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • ✓ 1 tsp cumin seeds & 1 bay leaf
  • ✓ 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • ✓ 1.5 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder & 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • ✓ 3 tbsp pure desi ghee or mustard oil
  • ✓ Fresh cilantro & 1/2 lemon juice for finishing

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Pressure Cook the Soaked Rajma

    Drain the soaked kidney beans and rinse thoroughly. In a pressure cooker or heavy pot, add the beans, 4 cups water, 1 tsp salt, whole black cardamom, and 1 bay leaf. Cook for 5–6 whistles on medium heat (or 25 minutes in an instant pot) until a bean crushes effortlessly between your thumb and index finger. Do not discard the flavorful cooking broth!

  2. Caramelize the Onion-Tomato Masala (Bhunao)

    In a heavy-bottomed kadhai, heat ghee over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle. Add finely minced onions and cook slowly for 8–10 minutes until deep golden brown. Stir in ginger-garlic paste and green chillies; sauté for 2 minutes. Add tomato puree, turmeric, coriander powder, Kashmiri chilli powder, and salt. Cook until ghee glistens on the edges.

  3. Simmer & The Bean-Mashing Technique

    Add the boiled rajma along with all of its reserved cooking broth into the masala. Stir well and bring to a vigorous boil. Take a potato masher or the back of a ladle and gently crush 1/4 of the cooked beans against the pan wall. The released bean starches naturally thicken the gravy, transforming thin water into a luxurious, creamy sauce.

  4. Final Slow Simmer & Ghee Tempering

    Lower the heat to low, cover partially, and let the rajma simmer for 15 minutes to allow the flavors to marry deeply. Finish with garam masala, fresh cilantro leaves, and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice. Serve steaming hot over basmati jeera rice with sliced red onions and mango pickle.
