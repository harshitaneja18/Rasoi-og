Star Anise (Chakra Phool)

Eight-pointed, rust-brown star pods with an intense, sweet licorice aroma — a quiet backbone of biryanis, garam masalas, and Chettinad curries.

Sweet & Licorice Biryani Spice Whole Spice

1. Botanical Profile

Star anise is the dried fruit of an evergreen tree native to southern China and Vietnam, also grown in Arunachal Pradesh. Its flavour comes from anethole, the same compound found in aniseed and fennel.

2. Culinary Uses in India

  • Biryani & Pulao: Added whole to perfume rice and meat stock.
  • Chettinad Masala: Roasted with pepper, fennel, and kalpasi (stone flower).
  • Garam Masala: Used in small amounts in many regional blends.

3. Using It Well

Cook’s Tip

Star anise is potent — one pod is usually enough for a whole pot. Fry it whole in ghee at the start of cooking and remove before serving.
