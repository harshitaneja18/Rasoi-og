Recipe Metrics

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 30 minutes
Total Time 50 minutes
Difficulty Intermediate
Servings 4 portions
Dietary Non-Vegetarian
Imperial Delhi Creation

Butter Chicken Masala (Murgh Makhani)

Invented in the 1950s at Delhi’s legendary Moti Mahal restaurant by Kundan Lal Gujral, Murgh Makhani turned leftover roasted tandoori chicken into global culinary royalty by simmering it in a velvety gravy of fresh ripe tomatoes, butter, cashew cream, and fragrant dried fenugreek.

Ingredients & Measurements

For the Tandoori Chicken Marinade:

  • ✓ 750g boneless chicken thighs (cut into 2-inch chunks)
  • ✓ 1/2 cup thick Greek yogurt / hung curd
  • ✓ 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ✓ 1.5 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder (for bright ruby color)
  • ✓ 1 tsp crushed black pepper & 1 tsp garam masala
  • ✓ 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice & 1 tsp salt
  • ✓ 1 tbsp mustard oil

For the Velvety Makhani Gravy:

  • ✓ 6 large ripe red tomatoes (roughly chopped)
  • ✓ 50g unsalted white butter (Makkhan)
  • ✓ 15 whole raw cashews (soaked in warm water)
  • ✓ 4 green cardamom pods & 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • ✓ 3 whole cloves & 1 bay leaf
  • ✓ 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • ✓ 1 tbsp dried Kasoori Methi (crushed)
  • ✓ 1 tbsp honey or cane sugar (to balance tomato acidity)

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Marinate & Sear the Chicken

    In a large bowl, whisk together the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri chilli powder, salt, lemon juice, black pepper, and mustard oil. Toss the chicken thighs until thoroughly coated. Marinate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight). Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill pan over high heat; sear chicken pieces for 4–5 minutes per side until charred edges appear. Set aside.

  2. Prepare the Tomato-Cashew Puree

    In a heavy saucepan, combine chopped tomatoes, soaked cashews, cardamom pods, cinnamon stick, cloves, bay leaf, 1 tbsp butter, and 1/2 cup water. Simmer covered for 15 minutes until tomatoes collapse completely. Discard the whole bay leaf and blend the warm mixture into a silky-smooth puree. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean bowl to achieve restaurant-grade glossiness.

  3. Simmer the Makhani Sauce

    In the same pan, melt remaining 35g butter over medium heat. Pour in the strained tomato-cashew puree. Bring to a gentle bubbling simmer for 8–10 minutes until butter begins glistening on the surface. Stir in Kashmiri chilli powder, garam masala, and 1 tbsp honey to balance the natural acidity.

  4. Unite & Finish with Cream & Kasoori Methi

    Gently transfer the charred chicken pieces and any resting juices into the simmering makhani sauce. Simmer on low heat for 6–8 minutes until chicken is tender. Rub dried kasoori methi between your palms to release its perfume and sprinkle into the pot. Swirl in heavy cream and remove from heat immediately.

Chef’s Secret for Authentic Restaurant Flavor

Do not skip straining the tomato puree! The velvety sheen of true Murgh Makhani depends on removing tomato skins and cashew grits. Also, toast your Kasoori Methi on a warm tawa for 20 seconds before crushing it between your palms—this awakens the delicate lactone esters that give Delhi butter chicken its unmistakable aroma.
