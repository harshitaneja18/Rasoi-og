Indian Spice Library

A comprehensive repository of the seeds, barks, berries, and roots that have shaped global trade routes and define the multifaceted flavours of Indian cooking.

Black Pepper (Kali Mirch)

Piper nigrum • Western Ghats

Ancient “Black Gold” of the Malabar Coast. Imparts sharp, penetrating piperine heat and pinene warmth.

Turmeric (Haldi)

Curcuma longa • South Asia

The sacred golden rhizome. Imparts deep yellow hues, warm earthy tones, and potent curcumin bioactives.

Green Cardamom (Chhoti Elaichi)

Elettaria cardamomum • Kerala

The Queen of Spices. Intensely floral, sweet eucalyptus aroma beloved in royal biryanis, chai, and kheer.

Cumin (Jeera)

Cuminum cyminum • Gujarat & Rajasthan

Foundation of everyday tadka tempering. Earthy, nutty, and transformative when dry-roasted on a tawa.

Explore Detailed Guide →

Coriander (Dhania)

Coriandrum sativum • Central & West India

Gentle, citrusy, and floral. Provides essential gravy-thickening body in curry foundations and spice rubs.

Explore Detailed Guide →

Cinnamon (Dalchini)

Cinnamomum verum • South India

Fragrant sweet inner bark. Essential in royal Mughlai kormas, dum biryanis, and whole khada masala.

Explore Detailed Guide →

Cloves (Laung)

Syzygium aromaticum • Coastal India

Intensely fragrant dried flower buds rich in numbing eugenol oil. Crucial for Awadhi dhungar smoking.

Explore Detailed Guide →

Mustard Seeds (Rai / Sarson)

Brassica nigra • Bengal & South India

Tiny dark seeds that pop in hot oil with nutty, wasabi-like pungent heat. The heart of Bengali fish and South Indian curries.

Explore Botanical Profile →

Star Anise (Chakra Phool)

Illicium verum • Arunachal & South India

Eight-pointed star pods with an intense licorice-sweet aroma. Secret to Chettinad chicken and Hyderabadi biryani broth.

Chettinad Signature

Kashmiri Chilli (Lal Mirch)

Capsicum annuum • Kashmir Valley

World-famous for vibrant crimson oil-soluble capsanthin pigments with mild, smoky warmth that won’t overpower delicate dishes.

Rogan Josh Essential

Fenugreek (Methi Dana)

Trigonella foenum-graecum • Rajasthan

Amber angular seeds with a pleasant maple-bitter aroma rich in sotolon. Balances sour tamarind and catalyzes golden dosa browning.

Nigella Seeds (Kalonji)

Nigella sativa • North & East India

Midnight-black triangular seeds rich in thymoquinone. Releases toasted onion and wild oregano perfume in Panch Phoron and naan breads.

Explore Detailed Guide →

Dry Mango Powder (Amchur)

Mangifera indica • North & Central India

Stone-ground from sun-cured unripe green mangoes. Delivers mouth-watering tartness to samosas and chaats without adding moisture.

Carom Seeds (Ajwain)

Trachyspermum ammi • Rajasthan & Gujarat

Pungent ridged seeds bursting with medicinal thymol. Indispensable for crispy samosa crusts, parathas, and digestive comfort.

Fennel (Saunf)

Foeniculum vulgare • Gujarat & Rajasthan

Sweet, licorice-scented green seeds — a panch phoron essential and India’s favourite after-meal digestive.
