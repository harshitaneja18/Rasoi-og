Coastal Treasure

Fish Curry

Fresh fish simmered in a tangy, coconut-rich gravy soured with kokum or Malabar tamarind — the everyday taste of India’s western coast.

1. Coastal Variations

Kerala’s meen curry uses kudampuli (Malabar tamarind) and coconut oil. Goan xitt kodi uses coconut and kokum, and Mangalorean versions add roasted spice pastes.

2. The Clay Pot

Kerala fish curry is traditionally cooked in an earthen pot, which spreads heat gently and is said to improve with age. The curry tastes even better the next day.

3. Serving

Eaten with steamed red matta rice, appam, or tapioca.
