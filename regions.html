The Six Culinary Realms of India

From the snow-fed Himalayan river plains to the spice-drenched tropical coasts, Indian cuisine is not a single entity, but an astonishing subcontinent of distinct culinary micro-climates.

Home > Regions
North India

North India

Characterized by royal courtly Mughlai and Awadhi heritages, clay tandoors, aromatic slow-cooked gravies, and rich dairy indulgence with pure desi ghee and makhana.

  • States: Punjab, Kashmir, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal, Haryana, Delhi
  • Signature Dishes: Chole Bhature, Rogan Josh, Butter Chicken, Dal Makhani
  • Staple Medium: Pure Desi Ghee, Butter, Mustard Oil

South India

South India

A tropical masterclass in fermenting rice and black lentils, tempered with crackling black mustard, curry leaves, fresh coconut milk, and sour tamarind.

  • States: Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana
  • Signature Dishes: Masala Dosa, Idli Sambar, Chettinad Pepper Chicken, Malabar Biryani
  • Staple Medium: Coconut Oil, Gingelly (Sesame) Oil
Explore South India →
East India

East India

Subtle, fragrant, and deeply connected to riverine waters. Renowned for pungent mustard oil, five-spice Panch Phoron, tender freshwater fish, and iconic milk sweets.

  • States: West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand
  • Signature Dishes: Machher Jhol, Litti Chokha, Dalma, Chhena Poda, Rasgulla
  • Staple Medium: Raw Pungent Mustard Oil (Kachi Ghani)
Explore East India →
West India

West India

A dynamic study in contrasts: arid desert resilience with sun-dried lentils (Dal Baati), delicate vegetarian sweet-sour Gujarati thalis, and fiery coastal Konkani seafood.

  • States: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa
  • Signature Dishes: Dal Baati Churma, Dhokla, Pao Bhaji, Goan Fish Curry
  • Staple Medium: Peanut Oil, Ghee, Coconut Oil
Explore West India →
Central India

Central India

The rustic heartland of India. Famous for slow-steamed baflas, Indori street breakfast poha, sweet corn preparations, and tribal forest produce including mahua and red rice.

  • States: Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh
  • Signature Dishes: Dal Bafla, Bhutte Ka Kees, Indori Poha, Chana Samosa
  • Staple Medium: Desi Ghee, Soybean Oil
Explore Central India →
Northeast India

Northeast India

Pure, organic, and rooted in indigenous foraging. Renowned for fermented bamboo shoots, medicinal herbs, smoked meats, alkaline khar, and the world-famous Bhut Jolokia chilli.

  • States: Assam, Manipur, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura, Arunachal, Sikkim
  • Signature Dishes: Assamese Masor Tenga, Manipuri Eromba, Naga Smoked Pork, Momos
  • Staple Medium: Mustard Oil & Minimal Oil (Steamed & Fermented)
Explore Northeast India →
