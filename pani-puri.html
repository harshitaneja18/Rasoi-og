Pani Puri

Hollow, crisp puris filled with spiced potato and chickpeas and dunked in tangy mint-tamarind water — eaten in one explosive bite.

1. Many Names

It is golgappa in Delhi, puchka in Kolkata, gupchup in Odisha, and pani puri in Mumbai — each city with its own filling and water.

2. The Pani

The spiced water blends mint, coriander, green chilli, tamarind, roasted cumin, and black salt. A sweet date-tamarind chutney balances the heat.

3. The Ritual

Vendors fill puris one at a time and hand them to customers standing around the cart, until they say stop.
